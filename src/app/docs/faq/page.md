## Is mapperoni only for maps?

No, it's a full-featured survey form builder too. You can use it to collect all kinds of data. It's a drop-in replacement for Google Forms, Typeform, Fillout, Jotform and other similar tools.

## I just want to edit maps. Is this the right tool?

Yes, mapperoni is a good fit if you:
- Want a simple, structured input for a small number of layers ✅
- Want to collaborate with several editors ✅
- Want to export to GeoJSON or take screenshots ✅

Mapperoni is not a good fit if you:
- Have a large number of layers, basemaps etc. Try QGIS, Esri or Felt instead.
- Want to perform advanced GIS analysis and data transformation. Try QGIS, Esri, or Felt instead.
- Want to embed web-maps on your own website. Try Leaflet or Mapbox instead.

## Can I upload my own geodata to the map?

This feature is on the roadmap but not yet implemented.

## Can I embed my project on other web pages?

Yes, you should be able to use an `<iframe />` element. Please see our documentation on [Sharing and privacy](/docs/sharing-and-privacy)

## What is collaborative and crowd-sourced mapping?

Asking a bunch of people to mark stuff on a map. It has other names as well

- VGI - Volunteered Geographic Information
- PPGIS - Public Participation GIS
- Neogeography
- Participatory Geoweb
- Community Mapping
- Multiplayer Mapping
