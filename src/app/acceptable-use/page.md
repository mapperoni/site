This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of Mapperoni and related services. By using our platform, you agree to comply with this policy.

## Permitted Uses

Mapperoni is designed for collecting feedback, conducting surveys, and gathering public input. Acceptable uses include:

- Public engagement and community feedback
- Surveys and questionnaires
- Planning and consultation projects
- Research data collection
- Internal team collaboration

## Prohibited Uses

You may not use Mapperoni to:

### Illegal Activity
- Violate any applicable laws or regulations
- Collect data without proper consent where required
- Facilitate fraud, money laundering, or other financial crimes

### Harmful Content
- Distribute malware, viruses, or malicious code
- Host phishing pages or deceptive content
- Collect sensitive personal data (health, financial, government IDs) without appropriate safeguards

### Abuse
- Harass, threaten, or intimidate others
- Collect information to discriminate against individuals
- Impersonate other organizations or individuals
- Spam or send unsolicited bulk communications

### Platform Integrity
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems or accounts
- Interfere with the platform's operation or other users' access
- Circumvent usage limits or security measures
- Scrape or harvest data in violation of terms

## Your Responsibilities

- You are responsible for all content and data within your projects
- You must have appropriate rights and consents for data you collect
- You must respond to valid data subject requests
- You must secure your account credentials

## Enforcement

We may investigate violations and take action including:

- Warning or requiring remediation
- Suspending or terminating accounts
- Removing content
- Reporting illegal activity to authorities

## Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use constitutes acceptance of changes.

## Contact

Report violations or questions to: support@mapperoni.com
