Mapperoni is designed to be self-hosted, easily white-labeled, and totally self-contained. 

The cost depends on how much customization you need. The basic deployment includes:
- Your custom domain.
- White-label branding (swap the mapperoni logo).
- Deployed on a private server of your choice (we can help).
- Regular upgrades alongside mapperoni.com.

This is typically suitable for most organizations, but we can also scale to meet the needs of large scale Enterprise customers.

Contact us & let's start planning your deployment!

## Why custom deployments?

Deploying your own instance comes with many benefits:
- Customize the domain & branding.
- Run it within a private network.
- Integrate directly into your data pipelines, ERP systems or other existing tools.
- Total control over the database and who has access.
- Request custom features.
- Enhance the platform for additional security, access, and compliance controls.
